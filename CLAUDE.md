# RafikiHub

## Design system

Follow the design system in DESIGN.md for all UI work:

@DESIGN.md

- Treat it as a style reference for RafikiHub's own brand. Don't use Spotify's name, logo or wording.
- The accent is RafikiHub orange `#FF8033` (text on it: `#121212`), not green. Use it wherever DESIGN.md says "Spotify Green".
- African character comes from the kente, mudcloth and beadwork patterns, and photography. Their earth tones (terracotta, acacia, indigo) stay inside patterns and decorative blocks, never in UI controls.
- SpotifyMixUI / CircularSp are proprietary and not available. Use a free geometric sans (e.g. Figtree or Inter via `next/font`) in their place.
